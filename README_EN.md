# Cash Flow & Financial Health Analysis

[🇪🇸 Español](README.md) | 🇬🇧 **English**

Portfolio case study focused on **cash flow, working capital and financial health analysis**, developed entirely with **simulated data**.

## Project Overview

This project analyses the financial position and cash generation capacity of a simulated company using information derived from the **Balance Sheet** and **Income Statement**.

The objective is to transform financial information into indicators, analysis and actionable insights that support business understanding and decision-making in areas such as **Finance Analytics, FP&A and Controlling**.

## Key Areas

* Cash Flow
* Working Capital
* Free Cash Flow
* Operating Cash Generation
* Financial Health
* KPI Analysis
* Balance Sheet and Income Statement Analysis
* Financial Performance and Variance Analysis
